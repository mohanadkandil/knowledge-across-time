Differences: language, knowledge and coverage

knowledge

Historical source claim

Britannica suggests the Prester John legend may originate from an Austrian priest named John who gained a throne in Asia, with the title inherited by his descendants, and connects the dynasty to the Tartar emperor Gerghiskan.

Modern source claim

Wikipedia states the origin of the idea officially originates from a letter received by Byzantine emperor Manuel I Komnenos in 1165 from "John, Christian Sovereign and Lord of Lords," describing rich lands in central Asia.

The two sources offer different accounts of the legend's origin; the historical Britannica text attributes it to an Austrian priest and dynastic inheritance, while Wikipedia points to the 1165 letter to Manuel I Komnenos.
