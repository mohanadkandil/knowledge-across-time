Modern source claim

Historical source claim

The Britannica article frames Leibniz's 1689 paper as a confused attempt to demonstrate Newton's elliptical planetary model, later corrected only in language, and describes a bitter priority dispute involving John Bernoulli and accusations of plagiarism.

Modern source claim

The Wikipedia Acta Eruditorum article states the calculus priority dispute began with Leibniz's May 1697 contribution and that the journal largely supported Leibniz's camp, while Philosophical Transactions supported Newton's.

The two accounts differ in the specific date (1689 vs 1697) and in the framing of the dispute, though both acknowledge a Newton-Leibniz priority conflict over calculus.
