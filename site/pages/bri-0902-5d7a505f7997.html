Modern source claim

Greenland is an autonomous Nordic nation that is a constituent country of the Kingdom of Denmark.

The Britannica text reflects 19th-century geographical uncertainty about whether Greenland was connected to North America, while the Wikipedia outline states modern political status and geography without addressing continental connection.
