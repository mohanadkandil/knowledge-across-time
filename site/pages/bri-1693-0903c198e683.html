Counterparts and relevance

strong

The Britannica entry on "Mint Marks" describes the historical practice of obliging mint masters and workers to place their own marks on coins so that each moneyer could be identified and held accountable, and it connects this to the trial of the pix. The Wikipedia mint mark article explains that mint marks identify where a coin was produced and were developed to locate problems such as underweight coins or debasement by dishonest officials, which parallels the Britannica rationale of identifying the maker of each coin.
