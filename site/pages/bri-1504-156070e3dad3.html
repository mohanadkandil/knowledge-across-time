Historical source claim

The Britannica text presents a nineteenth-century view that civil liberty was "the beauty discovery of modern themes, was then unknow" in ancient Athens, and that political equality was the only liberty employed in ancient democracies.

Modern source claim

Wikipedia describes Athenian democracy as a developed system with liberty, equality, and security, and notes that it was not the first or only Greek democracy.

The historical encyclopedia reflects an older interpretation that ancient Athens lacked civil liberty, whereas the modern article presents Athenian democracy as a robust system focused on liberty and equality.
