The supplied Wikipedia passages on vis viva and dynamics cover the same historical controversy but from a modern perspective, affirming that vis viva depends on the square of velocity and was absorbed into energy theory. knowledge

Historical source claim

The Britannica author argues that forces are proportional to velocities, not the squares of velocities, and criticizes Leibnizian doctrine.

Modern source claim

Wikipedia states that vis viva was dependent on the square of the velocities and was eventually absorbed into the modern theory of energy/kinetic energy.

The historical text takes a position opposing the square-of-velocity measure, whereas the modern source describes that measure as the historically influential vis viva concept later incorporated into energy theory.
