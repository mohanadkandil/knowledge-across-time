The historical Britannica entries and the pinned Wikipedia corpus share references to Alsace and alquifou/alquier, but differ in scope and detail. The Britannica text describes Alsace as a former province of France with German origins, mineral resources, and linguistic details, while the Wikipedia disambiguation page identifies Alsace as a cultural and historical region in eastern France and former administrative région without those specifics. The alquifou entry in Britannica closely parallels the Wikipedia description of alquifou as a lead ore from Cornwall used in pottery glazing.
