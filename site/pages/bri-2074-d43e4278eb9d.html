Summary

The historical Britannica passage is a mathematical treatment of torsional oscillation, including the proportionality of torsional force to angle of torsion and the effect of retarding forces on amplitude. The available Wikipedia passages concern torsion pendulum clocks and damping in general terms. There is a partial thematic match: both describe oscillatory motion driven or restored by torsion and diminished by retarding/damping influences. However, the sources differ in language, focus (historical symbolic derivation versus modern horological description), and coverage, and the OCR corruption of the Britannica formulas prevents precise knowledge-level comparison.

partial

The Wikipedia article on torsion pendulum clocks describes a torsion pendulum as a weighted disk or wheel suspended by a thin wire or ribbon, oscillating by twisting the torsion spring, which corresponds to the Britannica discussion of torsion, oscillation, and the force of torsion proportional to the angle of torsion. However, the Wikipedia text is a modern practical description of clocks, while the Britannica text is a dense historical mathematical derivation with OCR-corrupted formulas, so the match is partial rather than strong.
