Summary

The historical Britannica passage discusses the universality of the biblical deluge, objections to confining humanity to part of Asia, the separate creation of animals and man, and post-flood animal distribution, citing William Cockburn's treatise on the deluge and a claimed ancient population 68,719 times the present. The supplied Wikipedia corpus includes a "Deluge" disambiguation page that identifies the term with the Genesis flood, a "Coëtlogon" disambiguation page matching a name in the text, and a scriptural geologist article mentioning William Cockburn's anti-geological writings. The sources overlap on the deluge concept and Cockburn, but the modern texts lack the detailed theological and numerical arguments found in the historical source, and the historical text's OCR corruption introduces uncertainty about exact wording.
