Historical source claim

Modern source claim

Wikipedia uses standardized terminology: "phonolite," "clinkstone," "struck," and "specific gravity."

The historical text reflects 18th-century spelling and OCR noise, while the modern text uses current mineralogical nomenclature.

knowledge

Historical source claim

Britannica presents the origin of basalt as unsettled, noting one theory that it is "the effect of fallen" (igneous) and another that it "can have been deposited from an aqueous solution" (Neptunist), and states that its limits preclude even reviewing the literary arguments.

Modern source claim

Modern geology classifies basalt as an igneous rock formed from cooled lava; the Wikipedia disambiguation page simply calls it "a common igneous rock."

The historical text records a live scientific controversy between vulcanist and aqueous/origin theories, whereas the modern treatment assumes the igneous origin.
