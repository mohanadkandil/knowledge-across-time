Similarities

The Encyclopædia Britannica is a general-knowledge English-language encyclopedia first published in Edinburgh, Scotland.

The Britannica grew quickly in size from edition to edition in its early history.

The 15th edition (1974) reorganized the Britannica into the New Encyclopaedia Britannica with Micropædia, Macropædia, and Propædia sections.

The 2010 version of the 15th edition was the last printed edition, spanning 32 volumes and 32,640 pages.

Since 2016, the Britannica has been published exclusively as an online encyclopedia at Britannica.com.
