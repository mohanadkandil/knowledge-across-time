Summary

The historical Britannica third-edition text and the modern Wikipedia excerpts both concern the Roman–Parthian Wars, including Trajan’s installation of Parthamaspates, Hadrian’s retreat to the Euphrates, wars under Vologases/Vologases IV, Roman attacks on Ctesiphon, Septimius Severus’s Mesopotamian campaign, and the eventual fall of Parthia to Ardashir I. The main differences are linguistic: the Britannica passage is heavily OCR-corrupted and uses archaic spellings, while Wikipedia uses standardized modern forms. Some Britannica details, such as the Vologes–Arabanus succession conflict and the Artabanus–Granalla marriage episode, have no clear counterpart in the supplied Wikipedia fragments, but this may reflect coverage limits rather than factual contradiction.
