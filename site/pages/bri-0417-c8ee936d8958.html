The historical Britannica passage describes the inhabitants, appearance, dress, and tattooing customs of Obtahete (Otaheite/Tahiti), including barkcloth clothing made from shrub bark and tattooing by skin puncture. The Wikipedia corpus confirms that Otaheite is the former name of Tahiti and provides general descriptions of barkcloth and tattooing, but lacks the detailed ethnographic account found in the historical source. The main differences are language/OCR corruption and coverage depth, not substantive factual contradiction.

Modern source claim

The difference is primarily orthographic and editorial, reflecting historical OCR noise and older usage versus modern standardized naming.

coverage

Historical source claim

Modern source claim

The Wikipedia disambiguation page only lists Otaheite as the former name of Tahiti and does not provide ethnographic detail.
