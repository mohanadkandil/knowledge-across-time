The Wikipedia corpus describes fluid theories of electricity as outdated historical theories, outlining Franklin's one-fluid theory and du Fay's two-fluid theory, their deficiencies, and their eventual replacement. Both sources agree that electricity was conceptualized as a fluid with excess and deficient states, but differ in that the historical text treats the theory as active mathematical science while the modern source classifies it as obsolete.
