The modern Wikipedia corpus corroborates the economic importance and India-China trade of betel, but adds extensive documentation of health risks, including carcinogenicity and oral cancer, along with regulatory bans. Coverage differs, as the historical text includes a heavily corrupted biographical entry possibly about Bettinelli, for which no clear modern counterpart exists in the provided candidates.
